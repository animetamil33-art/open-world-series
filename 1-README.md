# Open World Series

An original open-world survival prototype for Android, built with Three.js.

## Download and play

1. Download the latest APK from **Releases** (open-world-series-v0.1.1.apk).
2. On your Android phone, open the downloaded file and allow install from this source when asked.
3. Launch **Open World Series** and play in landscape.

## How to play

- **Move**: left side of the screen - touch and drag the joystick.
- **Look around**: drag on the right side of the screen.
- **CHOP**: tap to gather wood from trees.
- **CRAFT**: tap to craft once you have enough wood.
- **Goal**: build the campfire to win.
